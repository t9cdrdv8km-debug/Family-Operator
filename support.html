Family Operator Support

Need help using Family Operator or have feedback? Contact the publisher at james@jackmedia.com.

What to include

Tell us what you were trying to do, what happened, and which device or ChatGPT experience you were using. Please do not email sensitive information about children or other family members unless it is genuinely necessary.

Product limitations

Family Operator provides conversational planning support. It may make mistakes and does not independently confirm that a task, booking, reminder or calendar event has been completed. Check important details and confirm any action in the relevant service.

For account, billing or ChatGPT platform issues, you may also need to contact OpenAI support through the official ChatGPT help centre.
