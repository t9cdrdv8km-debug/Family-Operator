Privacy Policy — Family Operator

Draft for review — not yet a final published policy.

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Publication condition: This draft assumes Family Operator has no separate database, account system, analytics collection or independent data collection. Verify the plugin package, hosting, integrations and actual operation before publishing this policy. Update it if those practices change.

Who operates Family Operator

Family Operator is published by James Kitchener. For privacy questions, contact james@jackmedia.com.

How the service works

Family Operator is designed to run as a skill or plugin within ChatGPT and to use information a user chooses to include in their ChatGPT conversation to help with family planning and organisation. The intended initial release does not operate a separate Family Operator database and does not independently collect family information.

Information handled by ChatGPT

Information you enter into a ChatGPT conversation is processed by the ChatGPT service. OpenAI's handling of that information is governed by OpenAI's applicable terms, privacy policy and account settings. Family Operator is not a substitute for reviewing those documents. OpenAI may process conversation information under its own policies and technical operation.

OpenAI privacy policy: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/

Information to avoid sharing

Only share information needed for the task. Avoid entering passwords, financial account credentials, government identification numbers, or unnecessary sensitive information about children or other people.

Third-party services and integrations

Some ChatGPT features or connected services may transmit information to third parties when you choose to use them. Their own privacy terms may apply. Do not assume that a task is private to Family Operator if you connect another service or ask ChatGPT to use one.

Retention, access and deletion

Because the intended initial release does not maintain a separate Family Operator database, Family Operator does not have a separate family-information account or database for users to access or delete. Conversation retention, deletion and account controls are managed through ChatGPT, subject to OpenAI's current policies and product features.

Children

Family Operator is intended to help adults organise household life. Adults should use care when entering information about children and should avoid sharing more than is needed.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if Family Operator's data practices change. The policy's date above should be revised whenever a new version is published.
