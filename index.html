Designed to work in ChatGPT

Family Operator is a conversational planning assistant, not a replacement for your judgement or for professional advice. You decide what to share and which suggestions to use. Availability of connected apps and actions depends on the ChatGPT features you have access to.

Privacy-first intent

The initial product is intended to operate within the user's ChatGPT conversation, without a separate Family Operator database or independent collection of family information.
